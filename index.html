Built for the nation

A single source of weather truth for India.

Problem statement SIH26069 from the Ministry of Earth Sciences and India Meteorological Department asks for a scalable platform that harvests weather intelligence from the open internet, stores it centrally, and gives analysts a live, filterable command view. Vajra is that operating system — designed for IMD, state disaster rooms, researchers and citizens.

📡

Collect everything that moves

APIs, public datasets, websites, #IMD and weather hashtags, photos, videos, GPS and citizen ground truth — ingested as a continuous stream.

🧠

Trust only what verifies

AI flags fake or misleading posts, drops duplicates, scores source reliability and auto-tags rainfall, flood, heatwave, fog, dust storm and wind events.

🗺️

See India, second by second

Windy-class animated layers, date / event / location filters, verification status and a national admin panel for disaster analytics.

Platform

From raw internet noise to a verified weather event.

Open-source big data building blocks, tuned for Indian monsoon, cyclone and heatwave realities.

01 IngestKafka / NiFi style collectors for APIs, crawlers, social firehose and citizen app.
02 EnrichTime, city, state, GPS, media hash, language and hashtag metadata.
03 VerifyLLM + CV models detect fakes, duplicates and untrusted sources.
04 ClassifyRain, thunder, flood, heat, fog, dust, wind — with confidence scores.
05 VisualizeNational lakehouse + real-time dashboard and admin workflows.
